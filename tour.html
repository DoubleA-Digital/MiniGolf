How to play

Three shots, three different skills
Full swing

Click once to start the backswing. Click again to set power: the landing ring follows you. Click a third time as the marker crosses the white line. Early slices, late hooks. Drag the dot on the ball to shape a draw or fade, or to hit it high or low.

Short game

Inside 80 yards, pick Bump & Run, Pitch or Flop, then set where it should land. Hold to swing back: a ghost ball travels toward your landing ring. Let go when they overlap. The dotted line shows the expected rollout.

Putting

Press R or the eye to read the green: dots flow downhill, faster on steeper slopes. Aim with a sideways drag, then pull down and release to putt. Pace is in feet of flat roll. Uphill putts need more.
